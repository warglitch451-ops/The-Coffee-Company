Our Story

Since 2012

A Passion for Quality.

Founded by a group of coffee enthusiasts in a small garage, The Coffee Company was born out of a desire to make specialty coffee accessible without compromising on quality.

We traveled to over 20 countries, meeting farmers and understanding the intricate science of soil, altitude, and processing. Today, we maintain direct-trade relationships with every farm we work with.
